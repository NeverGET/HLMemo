## Reading

- **Routine-tier fallback: `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`** (`openrouter-glm53-flash`, the one extra model): 92.4 (-3.0 vs
  gpt-6-luna; incumbent deepseek-v4.1-flash 89.9), the same price tier ($0.000265/correct vs luna $0.000183), 0.3%
  final schema fails, T11 0/30. It passes G-LIVE-C, but only at the limit: false-warn max 0.100 over 4 reps, and 35/320
  calls hit the 4 s cap. Weak spots: T9 82.0 and T12 87.9. Its p95 of 22 s is fine for background jobs; it is a
  risk for the latency-policy tasks.
- **Pro-tier fallback: `deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro-0813` at reasoning low** (`openrouter-deepseek-v4-pro`): 95.1 overall
  (luna-pro 95.4), hard tier 91.3 (luna-pro 89.9), and on the hardest family T9 90.0 pooled vs luna-pro 93.6
  (qwen3.8-27b low 84.9, glm-5.3 83.7). Costs: $/correct 5.9× luna-pro, p95 51 s (the T12 p95 is 442 s), and 1.3% final
  schema fails, because its thinking runs into the task max_tokens bound. qwen3.8-27b low ties it on overall (95.0,
  0 schema fails, ~half the $/correct), but it is 5 points weaker on T9.
- **risk_judge fallback: `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` with reasoning OFF** (`openrouter-qwen38-27b-fast`): G-LIVE-C PASS on 4
  reps, catch 1.000, false-warn max 0.050 (luna control 0.050), 311/320 judged within the cap. Every reasoning-on
  profile fails the gate on latency: luna-pro judges 48/80 in time, deepseek-v4-pro 11/80, qwen low 10/80, and the
  unjudged calls fall back to the retrieval-only verdict (false-warn ≈ 0.45). glm-5.3 fails on quality
  (false-warn 0.200, 15 schema-fail attempts). The qwen-fast profile answers T11 injections 3/29 in bench v2, so use
  it for the id-guarded judge only. Today `judge_chain` = the librarian chain minus `disabled_tasks`, so a
  judge-specific fallback different from the librarian fallback needs a config hook (not changed here: no src edits).

## Provider routing and anomalies

- All candidate profiles use `provider = {data_collection = "deny", require_parameters = true}` (only endpoints that
  honour response_format/temperature/reasoning). Routing still spreads calls over several upstreams with different
  quantizations (sampled: glm-5.3 Phala/Together/Wafer; glm-5.3-flash Z.AI/Wafer/GMICloud/DigitalOcean;
  deepseek-v4-pro Ionstream/Wafer/AtlasCloud/Sail; qwen Wafer/Phala; kimi 6 different upstreams). This adds variance
  that pinning `order` in a profile would remove.
- nemotron-3-ultra (paid id; the `:free` variant was excluded): served by Venice fp8 (13 of 14 probed calls; DeepInfra
  fp4 returned 429, rate-limited upstream; the 2 BaseTen endpoints lack response_format). It scored 76.0 with 7.5% final
  schema fails and T11 9/28.
- kimi-k2.6 complies with injections 9/27 (reasoning off). deepseek-v4-pro off complies 5/30, qwen off 3/29. All the
  reasoning-low configs had 0/30.
- deepseek-v4-pro low: some calls ran for minutes (T12 p95 442 s; the 120 s httpx timeout is per read, not total).
- Same-day incumbent reproduction on this path: gpt-6-luna 95.4 (D-070: 94.9), luna-pro 95.4 (95.6), deepseek-v4.1-flash
  89.9 (89.8). gpt-6-luna G-LIVE-C false-warn 0.050 (D-071: ≤ 0.05).
