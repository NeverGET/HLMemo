**Verdict: NO-GO for promotion as written.** The design should reduce some false closes, but several rules can strand answers or preserve stale retrieval results. This is a read-only static review; I ran no gates.

2. **Fact-level supersession:** Quote coverage does not prove that every claim in a sentence is outdated. Ignoring partial links in `superseded_among` also preserves the old hit, so G-E-TEMP may remain at 8/15. 3. **Action tier:** `same_subject ∧ ¬conflict` cannot distinguish duplicate from refinement; word overlap can miss translations and accept shared-topic false positives. Keep semantic duplicates as questions, require a verifier to test claim equivalence versus added detail, and calibrate *automatic* actions separately. For high-impact disagreement, follow D-067’s no-proposal rule rather than merely downgrading to a question.

4. **Refine direction:** Concrete-token supersets can add an unrelated attribute, and `recorded_at` is not evidence of which claim refines which.
